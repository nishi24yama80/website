I am a Predoctoral Research Professional at the [Energy Policy Institute at the University of Chicago (EPIC)](https://epic.uchicago.edu) and the [Becker Friedman Institute](https://bfi.uchicago.edu/), where I work with Professor Koichiro Ito. I am the inaugural [Brandt Research Fellow](https://climate.uchicago.edu/news/tomoki-nishiyama-awarded-inaugural-brandt-research-fellowship/) at EPIC. Before coming to Chicago, I studied economics at the Graduate School of Economics at the University of Tokyo.

My research focus is mechanism design for energy and environmental problems using both structural estimation and causal inference. In particular, I am interested in how markets for natural resources, electricity, and carbon emissions can be designed to allocate resources sustainably, efficiently, and equitably.

I plan to start a PhD in economics in summer 2027.

Research interests
------
* Environmental and energy economics
* Development economics
* Industrial organization
* Market and mechanism design
